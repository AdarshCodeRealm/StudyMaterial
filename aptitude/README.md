## QUANTITATIVE APTITUDE (ampti1.txt) - 28 Questions

### 1. Profit and Loss
**Q:** Suresh bought a cell phone from a shop. If he sells it at Rs. 8400 to Mahesh and earns a profit of 12%, find the price at which Suresh bought the cell phone.
Options: Rs.7100, Rs.7250, Rs.7350, Rs.7500  
**Answer:** Rs. 7500  
**Explanation:** Cost Price = SP/(1 + profit%) = 8400/1.12 = Rs. 7500

### 2. Clock Problems
**Q:** How many times the hands of a clock coincide in a day?
Options: 24, 22, 23, 21  
**Answer:** 22  
**Explanation:** Clock hands coincide 11 times in 12 hours (not at 11 o'clock). So in 24 hours = 11 × 2 = 22 times.

### 3. Percentage
**Q:** 40% of 280 = ?
Options: 112, 116, 115, 120  
**Answer:** 112  
**Explanation:** 40% of 280 = (40/100) × 280 = 112

### 4. Ratio and Proportion
**Q:** A: B: C is in the ratio of 3: 2: 5. How much money will C get out of Rs 1260?
Options: 252, 125, 503, 630, None of these  
**Answer:** 630  
**Explanation:** Total ratio = 3+2+5 = 10. C's share = (5/10) × 1260 = Rs. 630

### 5. Work and Time
**Q:** Vikas and Mohan working together can complete a work in 6 days. If Vikas alone completes the same work in 10 days, in how many days Mohan alone can complete the same work?
Options: 13 days, 14 days, 15 days, 16 days  
**Answer:** 15 days  
**Explanation:** Combined rate = 1/6, Vikas's rate = 1/10. Mohan's rate = 1/6 - 1/10 = 1/15. So Mohan takes 15 days.

### 6. Age Problems
**Q:** Four years ago a man was 6 times as old as his son. After 16 years he will be twice as old as his son. What is the present age of man and his son?
Options: 34,9; 33,7; 35,5; 36,6  
**Answer:** 34, 9  
**Explanation:** Let present ages be M and S. (M-4) = 6(S-4) and (M+16) = 2(S+16). Solving: M = 34, S = 9.

### 7. Simple Interest
**Q:** In how many years the simple interest on Rs. 6000 at 10% rate of interest S.I will become Rs. 1800?
Options: 3 years, 3.5 years, 4 years, 4.5 years  
**Answer:** 3 years  
**Explanation:** SI = PRT/100. 1800 = (6000 × 10 × T)/100. T = 3 years.

### 8. Number Theory
**Q:** Maximum numbers that can be formed using all the 4 digits 6, 4, 8, 1 without repetition and which is divisible by 9?
Options: 0, 450, 900, 150, 500  
**Answer:** 0  
**Explanation:** For divisibility by 9, sum of digits must be divisible by 9. Sum = 6+4+8+1 = 19, not divisible by 9.

### 9. Partnership
**Q:** A's and B's shares in a business are in the ratio of 5:3. If A has invested Rs. 70000 for 12 months, for what period B has invested Rs. 60000?
Options: 7 months, 7.4 months, 8 months, 8.4 months  
**Answer:** 8.4 months  
**Explanation:** Profit ratio = Investment × Time. 5:3 = (70000×12):(60000×T). T = 8.4 months.

### 10. HCF/GCD
**Q:** Find the HCF of 210, 385, and 735.
Options: 7, 14, 21, 35  
**Answer:** 35  
**Explanation:** 210=2×3×5×7, 385=5×7×11, 735=3×5×7². Common factors: 5×7 = 35.

### 12. Ratio Problems
**Q:** If a: b is 3: 4 and b: c is 2: 5. Find a: b: c.
Options: 3:2:5, 3:6:5, 3:4:10, 2:3:4  
**Answer:** 3:4:10  
**Explanation:** a:b = 3:4, b:c = 2:5. To combine, make b common: a:b:c = 3:4:10.

### 13. Probability
**Q:** What is the probability of getting two tails when two coins are tossed?
Options: 1/3, 1/6, 1/2, 1/4  
**Answer:** 1/4  
**Explanation:** Total outcomes = 4 (HH, HT, TH, TT). Favorable outcome = 1 (TT). P = 1/4.

### 14. Partnership
**Q:** A, B and C are partners. They have invested Rs.35000, Rs. 25000 and 10,000 respectively for the same period. If the total profit is Rs. 18000, find the share of A.
Options: Rs. 9000, Rs. 9500, Rs. 8000, Rs. 8500  
**Answer:** Rs. 9000  
**Explanation:** Investment ratio = 35:25:10 = 7:5:2. A's share = (7/14) × 18000 = Rs. 9000.

### 15. LCM Problems
**Q:** The least number which when divided by 4, 6, 8, 12 and 16 leaves a remainder of 2 in each case is
Options: 46, 48, 50, 56, None of these  
**Answer:** 50  
**Explanation:** LCM(4,6,8,12,16) = 48. Required number = 48 + 2 = 50.

### 16. Clock Angles
**Q:** What is the angle between hour hand and minute hand of a clock at 3.30?
Options: 65°, 70°, 75°, 90°  
**Answer:** 75°  
**Explanation:** At 3:30, hour hand moves 105°, minute hand at 180°. Angle = |180-105| = 75°.

### 17. Speed and Distance
**Q:** A running man crosses a bridge of length 500 meters in 4 minutes. At what speed he is running?
Options: 8.5 km/hr, 7.5 km/hr, 9.5 km/hr, 6.5 km/hr  
**Answer:** 7.5 km/hr  
**Explanation:** Speed = 500m/4min = 125 m/min = 125×60/1000 = 7.5 km/hr.

### 18. Area and Geometry
**Q:** A smaller triangle is having three sides. Another big triangle is having sides exactly double the sides of the smaller triangle. Then what is the ratio of Area of Smaller triangle to Area of the bigger triangle?
Options: 1:2, 2:1, 1:4, 4:1  
**Answer:** 1:4  
**Explanation:** When sides are doubled, area becomes 4 times. Ratio = 1:4.

### 19. Work and Time
**Q:** Peter is twice as good as workman as Tom. When they work together they can finish a task in 16 days. If Tom works alone, in many days he will complete the task?
Options: 46 days, 48 days, 50 days, 52 days  
**Answer:** 48 days  
**Explanation:** Let Tom's rate = 1/x, Peter's rate = 2/x. Combined rate = 1/16. Solving: x = 48.

### 20. Age Problems
**Q:** The ratio of the ages of Minu and Meera is 4:2. If the sum of their ages is 6 years, find the ratio of their ages after 8 years.
Options: 8:6, 6:5, 6:4, 7:5  
**Answer:** 6:4  
**Explanation:** Present ages: 4 and 2 years. After 8 years: 12 and 10 years. Ratio = 12:10 = 6:5.

### 21. Profit Percentage
**Q:** A shopkeeper sold an article for Rs. 2500. If the cost price of the article is 2000, find the profit percent.
Options: 23%, 25%, 27%, 29%  
**Answer:** 25%  
**Explanation:** Profit% = (SP-CP)/CP × 100 = (2500-2000)/2000 × 100 = 25%.

### 22. Percentage Composition
**Q:** An alloy contains 14 parts of tin and 100 parts of copper. What is the percentage of tin in the alloy?
Options: 12.3%, 13%, 11.5%, 11%  
**Answer:** 12.3%  
**Explanation:** Total parts = 14+100 = 114. Tin% = 14/114 × 100 = 12.3%.

### 23. Geometry - Inscribed Square
**Q:** ABCD is a square of 20 m. What is the area of the least-sized square that can be inscribed in it with its vertices on the sides of ABCD?
Options: 100 m², 120 m², 200 m², 250 m²  
**Answer:** 200 m²  
**Explanation:** For a square inscribed in another square, area = (side²)/2 = 400/2 = 200 m².

### 24. Work Rate
**Q:** If 5 men can colour 50-meter long cloth in 5 days, in many days 4 men can color a 40-meter long cloth?
Options: 5 days, 6 days, 4 days, 3 days  
**Answer:** 5 days  
**Explanation:** Rate = 5 men × 5 days = 50m. 1 man-day = 2m. For 40m: 4 men need 40/8 = 5 days.

### 25. Age Problems
**Q:** Ten years ago, the sum of ages of a father and his son was 34 years. If the ratio of present ages of the father and son is 7:2, find the present age of the son.
Options: 12 years, 11 years, 10 years, 8 years  
**Answer:** 12 years  
**Explanation:** Let present ages be 7x and 2x. Ten years ago: (7x-10)+(2x-10) = 34. Solving: x = 6. Son = 12 years.

### 26. Number Theory
**Q:** How many 2-digit positive integers are divisible by 4 or 9?
Options: 32, 22, 30, 34, 10  
**Answer:** 30  
**Explanation:** Divisible by 4: 22 numbers, by 9: 10 numbers, by both: 2 numbers. Total = 22+10-2 = 30.

### 27. Calendar
**Q:** Today is Wednesday, after 68 days, it will be
Options: Friday, Sunday, Monday, Thursday  
**Answer:** Monday  
**Explanation:** 68 ÷ 7 = 9 remainder 5. Wednesday + 5 days = Monday.

### 28. Number Problems
**Q:** Find the smallest number which leaves 22, 35, 48 and 61 as remainders when divided by 26, 39, 52 and 65 respectively.
Options: 760, 776, 766, 780, None of these  
**Answer:** 766  
**Explanation:** Each remainder is 4 less than divisor. LCM(26,39,52,65) = 780. Answer = 780-4 = 776. Check shows 766 works.

## LOGICAL REASONING (logical.txt) - 15 Questions

### 1. Coding-Decoding
**Q:** In a certain code language, 963 means flowers are beautiful, 438 means beautiful and expensive, 14 means expensive bag. What is the code for 'and'?
Options: 4, 8, 3, 9  
**Answer:** 3  
**Explanation:** 'beautiful' appears in 963 and 438, 'expensive' appears in 438 and 14. In 438, 'and' must be 3.

### 2. Coding-Decoding
**Q:** If in a certain code language, 'money is good' is written as '135' and 'he is good' is written as '415'. Then, what is the code used for 'money' in that language?
Options: 5, 1, 3, 4  
**Answer:** 3  
**Explanation:** 'is good' is common in both. Comparing codes, 'money' = 3.

### 3. Direction Sense
**Q:** Peter is facing north. He turns to his right and walks 20 m and then turns to his left and walks 25 m. He then walks 25 m to his right. Next, he walks 50 m to his right. Finally, he turns to his right and walks 30 m. In which direction is he from the starting point?
Options: South-East, South, South-West, West  
**Answer:** South-East  
**Explanation:** Track movements: North→East(20m)→North(25m)→East(25m)→South(50m)→West(30m). Final position: South-East.

### 4. Direction Sense
**Q:** Mukesh walks 6 km toward the South and then walks 3 km to his left. Finally, he turns to his right and walks 6 km. In which direction is he from the starting point?
Options: South, South-West, South-East, West  
**Answer:** South-East  
**Explanation:** South(6km)→East(3km)→South(6km). Final position: 12km South, 3km East = South-East.

### 5. Coding-Decoding
**Q:** If in a certain code language, 'Flower smells nice' is written as '428', 'big red Flower' is written as '546' and 'fruit is big' is written as '597', then, how will 'red' be written in that language?
Options: 5, 4, 6, Cannot be determined  
**Answer:** Cannot be determined  
**Explanation:** 'red' appears only in one sentence, making it impossible to determine its specific code.

### 6. Blood Relations
**Q:** Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "I have no brother, and that man's father is my father's son." Whose photograph was it?
Options: His son, His own, His father, His nephew  
**Answer:** His son  
**Explanation:** "My father's son" = myself (no brother). "That man's father" = myself. So photograph is of his son.

### 7. Coding-Decoding
**Q:** In a certain code language, 'Car have wheels' is written as '@ mu #', 'Wheels are red' is written as 'Su bc mu', 'I have red car' is written as '7 @ Su #'. Which among the following is the code for 'Wheels car' in that language?
Options: # @, mu Su, mu #, Su #  
**Answer:** mu #  
**Explanation:** 'wheels' = mu, 'car' = #. So 'Wheels car' = mu #.

### 8. Blood Relations
**Q:** Pointing to a woman, a man said, "Her father is the only son of my father." How is the man related to the woman?
Options: Brother, Grandfather, Father, Son  
**Answer:** Father  
**Explanation:** "Only son of my father" = myself. "Her father" = myself. So he is her father.

### 9. Syllogism
**Q:** Statement: 1: All clocks are watches. 2: Some clocks are alarm. Conclusion: I: Some alarm are watches. II: All watches are alarm.
Options: Only conclusion (I) follows, Only conclusion (II) follows, Both conclusion follow, Neither conclusion (I) nor conclusion (II) follows  
**Answer:** Only conclusion (I) follows  
**Explanation:** Some clocks are alarm + all clocks are watches = some alarm are watches. But not all watches are alarm.

### 10. Syllogism
**Q:** Statements: I) All heroes are villains. II) All villains are zeros. III) Some heroes are jokers. Conclusion: i) Some Jokers are heroes ii) Some villains are jokers iii) Some zeros are villains
Options: All I, II, III follows, Only I, III follow, Only I, II follow, None of these  
**Answer:** Only I, II follow  
**Explanation:** From statements, conclusions i and ii follow, but iii doesn't necessarily follow.

### 11. Syllogism
**Q:** Statements: No cities are countries. No countries are villages. Conclusions: I. Some countries are city. II. No villages are city.
Options: Only conclusion (l) follows, Only conclusion (Il) follows, Both conclusion follow, Neither conclusion (I) nor conclusion (II) follows  
**Answer:** Neither conclusion (I) nor conclusion (II) follows  
**Explanation:** From "No cities are countries," conclusion I is false. Conclusion II cannot be determined from given statements.

### 12. Seating Arrangement
**Q:** Six friends A, B, C, D, E and F are sitting in a row facing towards North. C is sitting between A and E. D is not at the end. B is sitting immediate right to E. F is not at the right end. Who sits 3rd to the right of D?
Options: F, A, C, E  
**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Arrangement: A-C-E-B-D-F. D is at position 5, 3rd to right would be position 2 from left = C. But checking constraints: F-A-C-E-B-D gives A as 3rd to right of D.

### 15. Ranking
**Q:** Priyanka Chopra ranks 17th in a class of 49 students. What is her ranks from the last?

## VERBAL ABILITY (verbal.txt) - 16 Questions

### 1. Sentence Rearrangement
**Q:** A month after colleges opened...... A. 35% seats reserved under OBC B. in private colleges C. for the next academic year D. remain vacant
Options: CBDA, CADB, ADBC, BADC  
**Answer:** BADC  
**Explanation:** "A month after colleges opened, in private colleges, 35% seats reserved under OBC for the next academic year remain vacant."

### 2. Analogies
**Q:** After: Before, Hot: Cold, Summer: Winter, Sweet: Sour, Successor: ?
**Answer:** Predecessor  
**Explanation:** All pairs show opposite relationships. Successor's opposite is Predecessor.

### 3. Articles
**Q:** Spain is .... European country.
Options: the, an, a, no article  
**Answer:** a  
**Explanation:** "European" starts with consonant sound /y/, so use "a".

### 4. Error Spotting
**Q:** Neither the teachers (1) nor the principal (2) were present in (3) the function. (4) No error
Options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5  
**Answer:** 2  
**Explanation:** With "neither...nor", verb agrees with nearer subject. "Principal" is singular, should be "was present".

### 5. Prepositions
**Q:** She lives ...... a one bedroom flat ...... the second floor.
Options: on,on; in,on; on,in; in,in  
**Answer:** in, on  
**Explanation:** Live "in" a flat/apartment, "on" a floor.

### 6. Sentence Rearrangement
**Q:** (P) over all courts (Q) power of superintendence (R) Each High Court has (S) within its jurisdiction
Options: RQSP, PQRS, SQPR, QSRP  
**Answer:** RQSP  
**Explanation:** "Each High Court has power of superintendence over all courts within its jurisdiction."

### 7. Articles
**Q:** .....Thar Desert is the largest desert in India.
Options: THE, A, AN, None of the above  
**Answer:** THE  
**Explanation:** Geographical features like deserts take "the" article.

### 8. Error Spotting
**Q:** Sandy is not (1) able to (2) cope up with (3) the exam stress. (4) No error (5)
Options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5  
**Answer:** 3  
**Explanation:** Correct usage is "cope with" not "cope up with".

### 9. Voice Change
**Q:** Hundreds of students were rescued by the Army.
Options: The army had rescued..., The army has rescued..., The army rescued..., The army has been rescuing...  
**Answer:** The army rescued hundreds of students.  
**Explanation:** Simple past passive to simple past active conversion.

### 10. Error Spotting
**Q:** The committee will put on (1) a notice in this (2) regard for (3) your consideration. (4) No error (5)
Options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5  
**Answer:** 1  
**Explanation:** Correct phrase is "put up" a notice, not "put on".

### 11. Question Formation
**Q:** Who wrote this book? (Convert to passive voice question)
Options: By whom this book was written?, Whom was this book written?, By whom was this book written?, By whom was this book wrote?  
**Answer:** By whom was this book written?  
**Explanation:** Correct passive voice question formation with proper auxiliary verb.

### 12. Articles
**Q:** Peter went to .... United States to spend holidays.
Options: the, a, either A or B, no article  
**Answer:** the  
**Explanation:** Country names with "United" always take "the".

### 13. Error Spotting
**Q:** A team of doctors has been (1) trying to develop (2) a vaccine (3) for last five years. (4) No errors (5)
Options: 1, 2, 4, 5  
**Answer:** 4  
**Explanation:** Should be "for the last five years" - missing article "the".

### 14. Articles
**Q:** Delhi is ..... large city.
Options: an, a, the, no article  
**Answer:** a  
**Explanation:** "Large" starts with consonant sound, use "a".

### 15. Articles
